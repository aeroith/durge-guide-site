Scope: home page (src/pages/index.html), mode Persuade. Tiers shares its world. Docs are Read, Screenshots Experience.

Audience and job: a BG3 player deciding whether they want this, wary of spoilers. They should understand what the mod does and how much each tier tells, in one screen. Success today is understanding, because there is no download. No interview happened: the owner's written brief stands in for it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page asks the mod's own question and lets you answer it the way the game does. It refuses a hero with a headline over a button and a screenshot below it.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black, oxblood, parchment, thin gold lines, blackletter names. Numbered dialogue lines with chevrons, a region banner with a compass node, a popup with a drop node, plaques with diamond ends, wax seals. Own CSS and SVG only.

STORY: You see a real note in the game's popup. You are asked how much you want to know. You answer, and the scene and the narrator change. You learn where else it hides, who it was written for, and that you can't have it yet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The scene full-bleed under the bar. The game's popup top left, untouched. A curtain of dark takes the right 63%, and on it, the question at up to 7.25rem on two lines, then the lede, three numbered lines, the narrator's answer, then the plaque 'Compare the three tiers', a link into the game page and 'There is no public download yet'. Under 1100px the words come first, and on a phone the scene is a crop of the note, in view under the answers.

FORM: The game's own conversation screen, carried by a real capture. Signature interaction: answering reveals the new scene from the note outward. Keys 1, 2 and 3 answer when focus is in the group.

seed: none (unavailable). Code-led: no image generation was available, and no comp round was run. Three compositions were rendered and compared at 1280 wide, at Normal and Extensive: a curtain at the right, a centred band across the bottom, and a two-column panel. The curtain won because it is the only one that keeps Extensive's second note readable.

Unresolved: a release date and download, which the owner will decide. The closing section's copy changes when that happens.
