FAQ

Where do I download it?

You can't yet. It isn't released.

Will it spoil the game?

Minimal and Normal are written not to. Extensive will, about whatever is in front of you, though its dialogue tags hold back anything that happens later. This site hides later-story screenshots behind bars for the same reason.

Do I have to play a Dark Urge Monk?

No, but that's who it's written for. A lot of the value is in Dark Urge choices and advice for that party, and you'll see lines that don't apply to you.

Does it work with a save I've already started?

Yes. Notes for places you've passed stay in reach if you walk back there.

Can I change tier in the middle of a run?

Yes, with the game closed. You can't switch from inside the game, because the text is built into the game's language file.

Do I need Script Extender?

No.

Does it work on Windows or Steam Deck?

It hasn't been tried. Nothing in it is specific to Linux, but the build scripts expect a Linux install.

A note didn't appear. Why?

Check that tutorials are on in the game's options. Each note shows once per profile, and it won't show during a fight or once its quests have closed. It may also have gone straight to the journal in a busy area.

Was this written by a person?

It was written with AI, with Claude drafting the walkthrough and notes from the sources listed in Credits, then checking them against the game's own data files.
