FAQ

Where do I download it?

You don't. Not yet.

Will it spoil the game?

Minimal and Normal are written not to. Extensive will, about whatever stands in front of you, though its dialogue tags keep later events to themselves. This site buries its later screenshots for the same reason.

Do I have to play a Dark Urge Monk?

No. But that's who it was written for. A note reads your party and whether you carry the Urge when it speaks, and says something else if you don't. Dialogue tags, quest lines and tooltips are fixed text, so some of those won't apply to you.

Does it work with a save I've already started?

Yes. Notes for places you've passed wait there, should you walk back.

Starting a second run?

Choose Reset Tutorials when the game offers it. Notes are tutorials, and the game shows each one once per profile. Refuse, and every note you've already read stays silent, even in the journal.

Can I change tier in the middle of a run?

Yes, with the game closed. Not from inside the game - the text is bound into its language file. For something quieter without a rebuild, use the loose page tucked in your ledger.

Will it slow the game down?

Not so you'd notice. See the numbers.

Can I remove it?

See Install.

Do I need Script Extender?

No.

Does it work on Windows or Steam Deck?

It hasn't been tried. Nothing in it belongs to Linux alone, but the build scripts expect a Linux install.

A note didn't appear. Why?

Check that tutorials are on in the game's options. A note shows once per profile, never during a fight, and not after its quests have closed. In a busy area, it may have gone straight to the journal.

Was this written by a person?

By AI, in the main. Claude drafted the walkthrough and notes from the sources in Credits, then checked them against the game's own data files.
