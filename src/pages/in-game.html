Where you stand

[[art hero-normal | hero-note-normal | Sazza's Cage with a short note at the top left, ending in There's more in your journal | Normal, in Act 1.]]

Walk near the right spot and the game's own side popup opens at the top left. Within ten seconds, it fades. On a controller, it offers a Dismiss button.

A note says what matters now, in a line or two. If there's more, its footer says so: 'There's more in your journal'. Many notes have nothing more to say, and no footer.

It waits until a fight is over, and until you've moved after loading a save. No more than two hang on screen at once. Pass another in the meantime and it follows a moment later.

Once all of a note's quests are closed, it falls silent.

It reads your party

A note looks at who stands with you before it speaks. Bring Astarion, and it names him. Leave him at camp, and it finds other words. Classes, skills, the gear in your packs and the dead are all counted.

It asks when the note fires, not before. Whoever is with you then is who it talks about. If nobody fits, it says less. Nothing here assumes the party you were told to bring.

A dozen doors

[[gate Act 1 and Act 2 places | narrator-scene | The game's own dialogue box with a narrator line and two answers, What do I need to know and I'll manage | Normal, at Waukeen's Rest.]]

Twelve places are worth stopping for. The song in the cove. The lift out of the dark. The road that only runs one way. Here the narrator takes the game's own dialogue box and asks what you want to know.

Ask, and it says the note. Say you'll manage, and it leaves you to it. Either way, the note waits in your journal. Nobody speaks aloud and the camera stays where it is. Minimal has no scenes.

A worn ledger

It sits in your pack from the start. Use it, and it opens like any book you find on a corpse. Its pages fill themselves as you go, in the order you were told.

It keeps your last 100 notes at Normal and your last 50 at Extensive, so it stays quick to open. The oldest pages go. The journal keeps everything.

The loose page

Tucked inside is a page with one line on it: 'How much do you want to know?'

Answer 'Little', and only what could be lost still speaks. The rest goes quietly to the journal and the ledger. Lesser pins and glints go dark with it. Answer 'Everything' and it all comes back. No rebuild and no reload. Minimal doesn't need one: there, every note is already about what could be lost.

One-line notices

Small finds get one line in the game's notice bubble. A couple of seconds, and it's gone. The full note waits in your journal. Notices queue behind the game's own banners, so none are lost. Minimal doesn't trouble you with small finds.

Treasure glints

[[split a1-normal-glint | The Risen Road with a burst of white flecks beside a buried chest | White flecks above a buried chest.]]

Come within 12 metres of a hidden treasure, and each of you rolls Perception, the game's own roll against DC 15. Pass, and it sparkles once. Fail, and it doesn't. You won't be asked again. There is no box and nothing to dismiss. Minimal has no glints.

The notes archive

Every note you've reached stays in the journal's Tutorials tab, along with the coordinates from your minimap. At Minimal, it keeps the words you saw on the ground. At Normal, some notes add a few lines that give nothing away. At Extensive, the detail follows, on the notes that earn it.

Quest lines

A mark and a line beneath a quest step. At Minimal and Normal, a deadline never names what's coming. It says only 'Time-limited', 'Before your next rest' or 'Before you leave this act'. At Extensive, it names the event.

Dialogue tags

A mark at the end of the line, inside the game's own dialogue box. Normal gives you only the mark: a star [[icon star]] for a good line, red [[icon mark]] for one to avoid. Extensive adds what happens the moment you click, and a skull [[icon skull]] on Dark Urge choices. Minimal leaves your words alone.

Item tooltips

Notable items end their tooltip with who they suit. On a controller, selecting the item shows it. Minimal leaves tooltips as it found them.

[[split a3-normal-tooltip | A shop on the controller layout, with a Thieves' Tools tooltip ending in Astarion | Normal, on a controller.]]

Map pins

Pins only appear within 160 metres of your party, so the map never floods. Named pins are loot. Pick the item up or open its chest, and the pin is gone. Extensive marks the story's steps as well. Minimal has no pins.

Points of no return

Near one of the 7 exits, each open quest you would lose comes up in a box. 'Next' shows the one after it. 'Enough' sends the rest away. Minimal and Normal only name quests you've already accepted. What becomes of them, they don't say.

[[gate Act 2 story | a1-minimal-camp-warning | Camp with a box reading Rolan needs you now. Neither rest nor leave. | The wording is real. The trigger was forced for testing - Rolan belongs to Act 2.]]

Camp warnings

Go to camp while a long rest would cost you someone, or a quest, and a box waits for the arrival banner to pass. It stays until you close it. There are 16 of these. Only Extensive says what would happen. Begin the rest, and whatever it hadn't said yet goes unsaid.
