Using it

There is nothing you have to open and nothing to learn. Play. Read what appears.

When notes appear

  • Each note shows once, when you first come near. Notes about a choice open sooner, about 14 metres out, because popups can't appear during a conversation.
  • A note reads your party when it fires. Whoever stands with you then is who it talks about.
  • Nothing appears during a fight.
  • After a load, nothing appears until you've moved. A popup beneath the loading screen would be lost.
  • In busy places, smaller notes skip the screen and go straight to the journal. One notice tells you it happened.
  • Once all of a note's quests are closed, it falls silent.

Reading the journal

  • Open the journal's Tutorials tab. Every note you've reached is listed under Map.
  • A note whose footer reads There's more in your journal says more there than it did on the ground. At Normal, that's a few hints that give nothing away. At Extensive, it's the other rolls, the other ways in and what it all costs.
  • Where gives the coordinates shown on your minimap.
  • Leave the tab's Show All box alone. It lists notes you haven't reached, and every version of each: the words for a party you don't have, over and over. At Extensive, it's a list of spoilers as well.

The ledger and the loose page

  • Both sit in your pack. Use them like any book or scroll.
  • A Worn Ledger keeps your last 100 notes at Normal and your last 50 at Extensive, in the order you reached them. At Minimal, it keeps them all. The journal always keeps them all.
  • A Loose Page asks how much you want to know. Little keeps only what could be lost on screen. The rest goes to the journal and ledger without a word, and lesser pins and glints go dark. Everything puts it back. Change your answer as often as you like.

Boxes and scenes

  • Near a point of no return, a box names each open quest you'd lose. Next shows the next one. Enough sends the rest away.
  • At camp, a box says what a long rest would cost. Close it to carry on.
  • At twelve places, the narrator asks in the game's dialogue box. What do I need to know? gets the note. I'll manage. doesn't, and it stays in the journal.

On a controller

  • Press the D-pad to dismiss a note. Its footer shows the button.
  • When there's more to read, the footer shows the way: the menu button, then the journal.
  • Tags and quest lines are ordinary game text, so they show the same way.
  • Select an item to see its tooltip.
  • The ledger and page are items in your pack. Select one and use it. Its box takes the pad's usual buttons.
