Using it

There's nothing to open or learn. Play as normal and read what appears.

What the icons mean

IconIn a note or quest lineOn a dialogue line
ArrowWhat to do here
Red markSomething you could missA line to avoid
StarA tip, at ExtensiveA good line
SkullA Dark Urge moment, at ExtensiveA Dark Urge choice, at Extensive. "Resist:" or "Take:" says which way the line leans.
DieA roll and its difficulty, at Extensive
Party, chestParty advice and loot, at Extensive

When notes appear

  • Each note shows once, when you first come near. Notes about a choice open a little earlier, about 14 metres out, because popups can't appear during a conversation.
  • Nothing appears during a fight.
  • After a load, nothing appears until you've moved. A popup under the loading screen would be lost.
  • In busy places the smaller notes skip the screen and go straight to the journal, and one notice tells you that happened.
  • Once all of a note's quests have closed, it stays quiet.

Reading the journal

  • Open the journal's Tutorials tab. Every note you've reached is listed under Map.
  • Where gives the coordinates shown on your minimap.
  • The tab's Show All box lists notes you haven't reached yet. At Extensive, that's full of spoilers.

On a controller

  • Press the D-pad to dismiss a note. Its footer shows the button.
  • Tags and quest lines are ordinary game text, so they show the same way.
  • Select an item to see its tooltip.
