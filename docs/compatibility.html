Known limits

  • English only. The mod edits the English text file.
  • Other mods that replace the English text file will conflict. Whichever loads last wins.
  • Mods that rewrite dialogue can lose tags, because a tag belongs to one specific line.
  • Game patches leave the text out of date until you rebuild. See Install.
  • The Upper City and the Iron Throne have no map pins. A pin in the Upper City stopped saves from loading, and the Iron Throne is left out to be safe. Their notes and quest lines still work.
  • Some scenes have no spot on the ground, such as camp scenes, so they only appear in quest lines.
  • Notices are short. The game only keeps them up for a couple of seconds, so camp warnings show twice.

How safe the wording is

The spoiler-free text at Minimal and Normal was rewritten line by line to a set of rules. No person has read all of it. A few lines are close calls - some next steps can't be named without a hint of what follows. Expect the odd slip.

Item mods

Tooltips and loot notes know about the item mods on the test machine, such as Fade's Equipment Distribution. Without those mods, some notes point to items you won't find.
